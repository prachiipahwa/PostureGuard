📐 Perfect Posture Guide

🪑

Spine Neutral

Keep natural S-curve. Avoid rounding lower back or arching excessively.

🦴

Head Position

Ears directly above shoulders. Chin slightly tucked. Avoid forward head posture.

💪

Shoulders

Relaxed and level. Avoid shrugging or one being higher than the other.

📱

Screen Height

Top of screen at or just below eye level. Tilt back 10–20° to reduce glare.

⏰

Take Breaks

Stand and stretch every 30 minutes. Walk for 2 minutes each hour.

🎯

Score Goals

Aim for 85+ consistently. Under 65 means active issues to address now.
